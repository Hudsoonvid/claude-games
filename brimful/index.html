A pocket world in a clay planter. Shape the land, pour real flowing water, raise forests and villages, call down storms — and mind the rim: whatever rises above it spills over the side.

  • ShapePick a tool from Earth, Life or Powers and hold the left button (or one finger) on the world.
  • Look aroundRight-drag or Option-drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, Shift-drag to pan. On a trackpad, swipe sideways to turn. Keys: WASD pan, Q E turn, Z X zoom, T G tilt. Touch: two fingers.
  • LifePlants need water nearby. Animals graze, hunt and multiply. Villagers fell trees, replant and build huts.
  • Meet themWith the Look tool, click a creature to see who it is and what it is doing; from there you can ride along and steer it. V switches to first person.
  • WorldsNine world types, any size, nine biomes. Save as many named worlds as you like.

Brimful

A pocket world in a clay planter. Pour things in, watch them settle, grow and collide — and mind the rim: whatever piles above it spills over the side.

  • Pour & buildPick a material and hold the left button (or one finger) on the world.
  • Look aroundRight-drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, Shift-drag to pan. Touch: two fingers, or the Look tool.
  • LifeGrass and trees need water nearby. Mossbacks graze tufts, minnows nibble waterweed, glimmers light up at night.
  • Ride alongChoose Ride along and click any creature to steer it yourself.
  • Keys1–9 tools · [ ] brush size · P pause · R rain · H help
